## Recovered official sources

- Asheville: current Accela planning layer, replacing obsolete development-location data. Major developments and individually screened Level I physical construction; no parking-only/downspout/tiny-addition padding.
- Charlotte: City public-facing transit-area development inventory. Completed projects excluded; proposed and construction statuses preserved. This is not the whole city pipeline.
- Wilmington: current 2026 capital-project dashboard. Distinct MUNIS projects grouped; legacy tracker has a November 2024 submission cutoff. Private development coverage still needs expansion.
- Winston-Salem/Forsyth: planning-board reviews and major subdivisions, grouped by exact docket and latest dated decision. Withdrawn cases excluded.
- Brunswick: current residential/commercial dashboards. Only explicitly reported units under construction establish construction; incomplete buildout alone does not. Undated commercial approvals remain excluded.
- Harnett: recent major preliminary subdivisions, substantive site plans and municipal development applications. Final plats, minor subdivisions and certificates excluded as independent markers.
- Pender: recent pending substantive subdivision/site-plan cases. Approval dates are not inferred from last-edited dates; stale/ambiguous cases remain out.
- Statewide: current NCDOT active contracts and future letting list cross-referenced to mapped STIP identifiers. Routine maintenance excluded. Future programs remain Proposed.

## Additional gaps requiring work

Onslow has a public project tracker, but its residential/commercial layers do not expose a current disposition. Wilson has development locations without sufficient status/approval-date evidence. Rocky Mount labels its interactive development map “coming soon.” These are source gaps to work around using planning agendas and credible supplementary reporting; they do not mean those places lack development.

Greenville, Concord, Jacksonville, Rocky Mount and Wilson remain priorities. Broader county research is also incomplete where the table shows a shortfall or only transportation coverage. No claim is made that every area has received a full substantive source audit.

## Preservation and validation

The import was rehearsed against database copies before live application. All original project rows, follows, accounts, duplicate decisions and classification candidates were compared and preserved. New additions are transactional, indexed, evidence-backed and idempotent. Uncertain matches enter the existing protected research queue. No existing projects were automatically merged or deleted.

Backups are in `server/data/backups/`. Research files and full raw government responses remain outside the client bundle. Public viewport queries and clustering continue to use SQLite spatial and search indexes.
